App name
MORGANT THIERRY

Short description
Track stock, orders, reservations, and compliance in one offline operations app.

Full description
MORGANT THIERRY helps daily operations teams keep core business tasks organized on a single Android device. Track inventory levels, flag low-stock items, create supply orders, manage reservations, and monitor compliance reports from a focused control board.

Records are stored locally on the device. The app does not require an account, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, location, contacts, camera, microphone, or external service access.
