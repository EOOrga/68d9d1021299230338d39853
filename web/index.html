Privacy by design

MORGANT THIERRY is configured as a local productivity tool. The app does not request Android permissions and does not use remote APIs.

  • No analytics, ads, or tracking identifiers.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or payment data.
  • Deleting app data or uninstalling removes locally stored records according to Android system behavior.
